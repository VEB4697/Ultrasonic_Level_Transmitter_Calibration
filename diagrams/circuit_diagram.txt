3. HARDWARE WORKING PRINCIPLE & SIGNAL PATH
===========================================
1. Ultrasonic Measurement:
   Arduino sends a 10 microsecond HIGH pulse to HC-SR04 TRIG pin. The sensor emits an 8-cycle 40 kHz 
   ultrasonic burst. The ECHO pin goes HIGH for a duration proportional to the distance traveled.
   Distance d = (Time * v_sound) / 2.
   Liquid Level L = Vessel_Height (100cm) - d.

2. Software Calibration:
   The raw measured level is passed through a moving average noise filter and transformed via the calibrated 
   Gaussian linear equation: V_out = (0.03964 * Level) + 1.02182.

3. Industrial Transmitter Signal Conditioning:
   Arduino outputs a PWM duty cycle on Pin 3 corresponding to V_out. A 2nd order RC low-pass filter converts 
   PWM to a smooth DC voltage. An LM358 operational amplifier in a constant current feedback loop drives a 
   transistor to generate a standard 4 to 20 mA industrial current loop.
